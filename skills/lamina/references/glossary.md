## Glossary

**Affordance** — Perceived and actual properties determining how an object could be used ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Affinity diagramming** — Clustering actor-walk and walkthrough evidence into themed findings ([Research Synthesis](../../lamina-research/references/research-synthesis.md), [Interview Documentation](../../lamina-research/references/interview-documentation.md))

**Applied research** — Research serving a specific real-world goal ([Research Scoping](../../lamina-research/references/research-scoping.md))

**Behavior-first design** — Method focusing on user goals, expectations, and aptitudes ([Product Behavior](../../lamina-product-behavior/references/product-behavior.md))

**Breadcrumb** — Secondary navigation showing hierarchy from homepage ([Navigation](../../lamina-ux/references/navigation.md))

**Causal research** — Post-launch cause-and-effect analysis ([Feature Discovery](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/feature-discovery.md))

**Cognitive effort minimization** — Pages and labels should not require unnecessary thought ([Content Design](../../lamina-ux/references/content-design.md))

**Competitive research** — Systematic analysis of alternatives and market landscape ([Competitive Analysis](../../lamina-research/references/competitive-analysis.md))

**Conceptual model** — User's mental story of how a product works ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Concise copy principle** — Halve web copy, then halve again ([Content Design](../../lamina-ux/references/content-design.md))

**Constraints** — Limits on possible actions ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Contextual inquiry** — Observing product behavior in repo and live UI ([Field Research](../../lamina-research/references/field-research.md))

**Deep-page navigation orientation audit** — Five navigation questions answerable in five seconds from any page ([Navigation](../../lamina-ux/references/navigation.md))

**Design requirements** — Capabilities a product must provide ([Requirements Definition](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/requirements-definition.md))

**Descriptive research** — Understanding context of a defined problem ([Feature Discovery](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/feature-discovery.md))

**Discoverability** — Whether users can determine what actions are possible ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Evaluative research** — Testing proposed solutions ([Feature Discovery](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/feature-discovery.md), [Usability Evaluation](../../lamina-evaluation/references/usability-evaluation.md))

**Featuritis** — Adding features without behavioral coherence ([Product Behavior](../../lamina-product-behavior/references/product-behavior.md))

**Feedback** — Communicating action results back to the user ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Generative research** — Exploratory research before problem is defined ([Feature Discovery](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/feature-discovery.md))

**Gulf of evaluation** — Gap between system state and user's ability to interpret it ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Gulf of execution** — Gap between user's goal and allowed actions ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Heuristic evaluation** — Parallel expert lens review on contract and live product ([Heuristic Review](../../lamina-evaluation/references/heuristic-review.md))

**Human-centered design** — Design loop centered on actor outcomes via contract and verify ([Design Process](../../lamina-ux/references/design-process.md))

**Implementation model** — How engineers think the product works internally ([Product Behavior](../../lamina-product-behavior/references/product-behavior.md))

**Mental model** — How users think something works based on experience ([Product Behavior](../../lamina-product-behavior/references/product-behavior.md))

**Mindless choices** — Unambiguous options requiring no thought to select ([Content Design](../../lamina-ux/references/content-design.md))

**Mistake** — Error where the goal or plan is wrong ([Error Handling](../../lamina-ux/references/error-handling.md))

**Perpetual intermediate** — Users who know basics but are not experts ([Feature Prioritization](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/feature-prioritization.md))

**Persona** — Archetypal user synthesized from research ([User Modeling](../../lamina-research/references/user-modeling.md))

**Primary persona** — The user the design must satisfy completely ([User Modeling](../../lamina-research/references/user-modeling.md))

**Product posture** — Product's behavioral stance relative to user attention ([Platform Posture](../../lamina-product-behavior/references/platform-posture.md))

**Research question alignment** — Cross-functional session to align on research questions ([Problem Framing](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/problem-framing.md))

**Right-sized research** — Matching research scope to decisions at hand ([Research Scoping](../../lamina-research/references/research-scoping.md))

**Satisficing** — Choosing first satisfactory option rather than optimal ([Content Design](../../lamina-ux/references/content-design.md))

**Scan-first layout** — Designing pages for rapid scanning, not careful reading ([Content Design](../../lamina-ux/references/content-design.md))

**Scenario** — Narrative describing persona achieving a goal ([Requirements Definition](../../lamina-product-discovery/references/requirements-definition.md))

**Signifier** — Signal communicating where action should take place ([Discoverability](../../lamina-ux/references/discoverability.md))

**Slip** — Error where goal is correct but execution fails ([Error Handling](../../lamina-ux/references/error-handling.md))

**Two-cycle diverge-converge process** — Discover, define, develop, deliver ([Design Process](../../lamina-ux/references/design-process.md))

**Usability testing** — Simulated actor walks on live product during verify ([Usability Evaluation](../../lamina-evaluation/references/usability-evaluation.md))
