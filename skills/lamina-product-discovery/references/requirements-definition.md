# Requirements definition

Write requirements as traceable product behavior:

- Promise Resources/Statements: outcomes the product must preserve.
- Actor, Entity, Operation, Workflow, Invariant, and dependency facts: how the
  product stays coherent.
- Scenario Resources: observable distinct risks and recovery.
- Decision Resources: confirmed choices, assumptions, and policy forks.
- Scope Statements: current in/out boundaries.
- Traceability Statements: promise-to-graph links.

Mark every node's criticality, provenance, confidence, and relevance. Keep stack and vendor choices out unless the user explicitly constrained them. Defer non-current behavior instead of padding the contract.
