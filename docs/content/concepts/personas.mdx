# Personas and Missions

Personas are stable Resources. Actors express authority; typed Statements connect Personas to Actors they can assume.

`lamina mission compile` queries all relevant Personas, workflow/operation closure, invariants, recovery paths, Surfaces, adapter capabilities, proofs, evidence, budgets, and isolation. It creates an independent Mission for every relevant Persona without a cap.

Persona interpretation is simulated. Only adapter-observed events become runtime evidence.
